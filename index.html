What's New

FinalGrade.io version history. Dates reflect the project's release timeline and the major features associated with each stage of development.

2026 • 26.x Series

26.4.30 — September 2026 LATEST

  • Cleaner category, assignment, and removal screens with search and selection counts.
  • Anchored overlay menus for Settings, Help, and Quick Actions, with keyboard navigation.
  • Categorized Settings navigation and a taskbar Save button that appears only for pending changes.
  • Missing and Cheated lock the score at zero and remember the previous score across saves. Removing the flag restores it.
  • Dropped preserves and locks the entered score until unmarked. Other flags do not change the score.
  • Student Assignments display flags beside scores while keeping the classic portal and assignment details.
  • Fixed bulk removal, stale secondary statuses, and category edit layering.

26.4.20 — September 2026

  • Changed Final Avg to a weighted enabled-progress calculation: Σ(grade × weight) ÷ Σ(weights). Four 100-weight periods divide by 400; 100 + 150 + 100 + 150 divides by 500.
  • Changed /0 grading to Infinite Campus-style extra credit: an entered score adds earned points without adding possible points, while blank /0 assignments stay neutral.
  • Assignments with Total Points left blank are excluded from calculation until a total is set, avoiding accidental extra-credit behavior.
  • Added automatic grade-flag parsing for I, M, T, LT, LD, D, L, CH, and CHT while preserving literal non-flag grades such as AB and E.
  • Expanded Student Assignments with grade flags, comment previews, and two-line assignment titles on narrow layouts.
  • Simplified assignment-change notifications to a generic “Assignment has been updated” message for names, due dates, statuses, comments, totals, and multipliers.
  • Reset Everything now clears notification history, unread counts, and related local notification state.
  • Default grade-category weights remain 0% for Final, Formative, Summative, and Uncategorized Assignment.

26.4.10 — September 2026

  • Introduced the Semi-Multiplier system so custom assignment multipliers add moderate weight without overpowering the entire gradebook.
  • Multiplier 0 remains excluded from grade calculation; multiplier 1 remains normal weighting.
  • Custom multipliers use 25% of their extra weighting. Example: 2× becomes 1.25× effective, 3× becomes 1.50× effective.
  • Expanded progress-report management, manual saving, unsaved-change warnings, Student Assignments, notifications, and progress-information sidebars.
  • Improved Student View assignment details, flags, teacher comments, sorting, string grades, and bonus /0 behavior.

26.4 — August 2026

  • Major Student View redesign based on a compact Infinite Campus-style grade portal layout.
  • Added 4.5 Week, 9 Week, 13.5 Week, 18 Week Progress, and Final Avg reporting structure.
  • Added due dates, teacher comments, status flags, multiplier exclusion labels, and assignment detail panels.
  • Reworked the top taskbar, persistent bottom navigation, MiniDiamond ownership branding, legal pages, and Poppins title typography.
  • Added category visibility rules, latest-to-oldest assignment sorting, same-date Z–A sorting, and string-grade display support.

  • Added stronger mobile/tablet layout handling and improved category/assignment interactions.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026 • Owner: MiniDiamond

1. Local Grade Data

FinalGrade.io stores gradebook data, preferences, student-view expansion states, and term-display settings in your browser using localStorage. The application does not require a MiniDiamond account or a grade-data server to perform its calculations.

2. What Leaves Your Device

The bundled app does not intentionally upload your gradebook records. If your browser loads external font or icon resources, those providers may receive ordinary network metadata such as your IP address and user-agent. Your entered gradebook values are not intentionally sent with those requests.

3. Educational Records

Do not enter protected or regulated student information unless your school, district, or organization permits use of a local browser tool for that information. You are responsible for device security, browser access, exports, screenshots, and backups.

4. Student Portal Styling

The Student View uses an academic portal layout inspired by common student-information systems. FinalGrade.io is an independent MiniDiamond project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Infinite Campus.

5. Source Protection

The single-file release may obfuscate client-side JavaScript to discourage casual copying. Browser-delivered code cannot be made truly secret, and the obfuscation must not be relied on to protect passwords, keys, or confidential information.

6. Changes

MiniDiamond may revise this policy as the app changes. The current “Last Updated” date will identify the active version.

Weighted Grade Calculation Explanation

Example (Weights in Percent):

Math: 80, Weight: 30%
Biology: 90, Weight: 50%
History: 72, Weight: 20%

Weighted Grade = (0.30 × 80) + (0.50 × 90) + (0.20 × 72) = 24 + 45 + 14.4 = 83.4

Example (Weights in Points/Hours):

Math: 80, Weight: 3 points
Biology: 90, Weight: 5 points
History: 72, Weight: 2 points

Weighted Grade = (3×80 + 5×90 + 2×72) / (3+5+2) = (240 + 450 + 144) / 10 = 834 / 10 = 83.4
